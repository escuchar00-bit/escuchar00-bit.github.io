Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 8, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Nantong Liangrui International Import & Export Co., Ltd. (“Liangrui”, “we”, “us”) handles information submitted through LiangruiHome.com.

Information we collect

When you submit an inquiry, we may receive your name, company name, business email address, product interest, target quantity, size or specification information, and any information you choose to include in your development brief. We may also receive basic campaign attribution information such as referring page and UTM campaign parameters when present.

How we use information

  • To respond to product, quotation and development inquiries.
  • To evaluate whether a requested knit blanket program fits our manufacturing and export capabilities.
  • To understand which marketing sources generate legitimate business inquiries.
  • To maintain business correspondence and follow up on requested services.

Inquiry form provider

Our inquiry form uses FormSubmit as a form-delivery service to transmit submitted information to our business email inbox. FormSubmit states that submissions may be retained temporarily for delivery and recovery purposes. You can review the provider’s privacy information at formsubmit.co/privacy.

Cookies and advertising technologies

At present, LiangruiHome.com does not activate third-party advertising pixels or analytics cookies. If we later add Google Analytics, Google Ads, Meta Pixel, LinkedIn Insight Tag or similar technologies, this policy will be updated and any legally required notice or consent mechanism will be added before those technologies are used where required.

Retention

Business inquiries received by email may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to respond, quote, develop samples, maintain commercial records, or comply with legal obligations. You may request deletion of an inquiry unless retention is required for legal or contractual reasons.

Sharing

We do not sell personal information. We may share inquiry information only with service providers supporting website form delivery or with relevant production and business personnel when needed to evaluate or fulfill your request.

Your choices

You may contact us to request access, correction or deletion of information you have submitted, subject to applicable law and legitimate business record requirements.
