BUYER FAQ

Before you send your brief.

Can you develop both oversized-loop and machine-knit chunky blankets?

Our production resources cover both directions. Please share a photo or reference sample so the yarn, construction, sample route and order requirements can be reviewed for your design.

What is the usual MOQ for a chunky knit blanket?

Our general guideline is around 1,000 pieces per style. The actual MOQ depends on construction, yarn, colors and packaging; qualified trial orders can be discussed.

Can you customize dimensions, colors and private-label packaging?

Yes. We can review yarn, color, size, weight, labels, retail packaging and carton marking against your buyer brief.

What do you need to provide a first quotation?

Please send a reference image or sample, target size and material, estimated quantity, intended packaging and target price if available. If your brief is not yet complete, start with what you have.
