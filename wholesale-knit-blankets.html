WHOLESALE BUYER FAQ

Answers before you place an inquiry.

What is your minimum order for wholesale knit blankets?

Our typical reference is approximately 1,000 pieces per style. MOQ is confirmed individually and depends on yarn, knit construction, color assortment and packaging. Smaller qualified trial orders can be discussed.

Can I mix several styles or colors in one wholesale order?

Share the proposed style and color breakdown with your total quantity. Feasibility and minimums are reviewed by construction and material rather than assumed across all styles.

Can you quote a target FOB price?

Yes. Please include a reference image, target size and weight, material preference, estimated order quantity and packing requirements. We can then review an appropriate cost direction; pricing is not fixed without a confirmed specification.

Can you develop a private-label wholesale collection?

We can discuss buyer-specified knit patterns, colors, labels, care tags, retail packaging and cartons. For additional details, see our private-label knit blanket development page.

How long do samples and bulk orders take?

Sample development is often around 7 days after requirements are confirmed. Bulk production timing depends on approved construction, quantity, material availability and the order schedule; we confirm it case by case.

Do you have ready stock or a fixed price list?

This is primarily a development-led wholesale and OEM/ODM offering, not a promise of ready-to-ship inventory. We quote against your intended product specifications and order requirements.
