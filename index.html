BUYER FAQ

The questions sourcing teams ask first.

What is your standard MOQ?

Typical MOQ is around 1,000 pcs per style. Qualified trial quantities can be discussed depending on construction, yarn, color and packaging requirements.

How fast can you develop a sample?

Typical sample development is around 7 days after construction details, yarn direction and size are confirmed.

Can you develop from an existing retail sample or image?

Yes. A physical sample, reference image, CAD, specification sheet or target cost can all be used as a development starting point.

Do you support private-label packaging?

Yes. Hang tags, wash labels, barcode labels, gift boxes, poly bags, vacuum packing and carton markings can be developed around program requirements.

What about testing and certification?

Testing and certification can be arranged according to confirmed buyer and retail-program requirements. Certification claims are made only when the relevant program is in place.

What information do you need for a first quotation?

Product type, size, material or yarn direction, target weight, quantity, packaging and—if available—a reference image or target price. If some details are not fixed, we can begin with the information you have.
